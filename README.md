## 🏗️ What I'm Building

### BigBoc — [bigboc.com](https://bigboc.com)

> *"Transformamos ideas en productos digitales que impulsan el crecimiento de tu negocio."*

A Colombian software studio shipping web apps, mobile apps, e-commerce, fintech and AI implementations for companies across LATAM. I lead architecture and the data/AI side of every product that leaves the door.

- 🚦 **[ChatVial](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/chatvial/id6793770088)** — conversational legal assistance for traffic violations, on-device UX
- 🔁 **[Swaplai](https://swaplai.com)** — habits, goals, and accountability as a social exchange

### Tabteca — [tabteca.com](https://tabteca.com)

> *"Construye más. Gasta menos."*

A curated directory of **110 verified services with genuinely usable free plans** across 14 categories — hosting, databases, AI/ML, auth, monitoring — plus a browser tab manager that restores your sessions across devices. No signup wall. No paywall. No detours.

### Open Source & Research

- 🛡️ **[Proxy-LLM](https://github.com/miguelcort/Proxy-LLM)** — a defensive layer between your app and the model: prompt-injection detection, PII redaction, request policy
- 🎓 **[ia_repository](https://github.com/miguelcort/ia_repository)** — Machine Learning course material, Universidad Distrital
- ☁️ **[bsg_aws_2024](https://github.com/miguelcort/bsg_aws_2024)** — AWS training decks I've delivered
